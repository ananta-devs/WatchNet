**Website/domain policies**
- Allow specific domains.
- Block specific domains.
- Domain category filtering.

**Reports**
- Domain-level activity summary.
- Device online history.
- Blocked domain history.
- Policy activity.
- Weekly reports.
- Profile activity summary.

Important technical rules:

DNS filtering can enforce domain-level policies only when devices use the managed resolver and the network prevents or limits bypasses.

VPNs, alternative DNS providers, DNS-over-HTTPS and encrypted client technologies may reduce visibility or bypass some controls.

For dependable enforcement, integrate with a supported router/firewall/gateway. Do not promise complete protection on unsupported consumer router firmware.

Prefer an established DNS filtering engine such as AdGuard Home or Pi-hole through a well-defined adapter. Evaluate a native DNS service only if it is technically justified.

Never decrypt HTTPS traffic or install interception certificates.

All family monitoring must be transparent, access-controlled and configurable. Maintain audit records for policy changes and provide data-retention controls.
